10 / GOOD TO KNOW

BEFORE
THE NIGHT.

The important details of this launch concept.

Is Night Shift a real event already? +

This website presents an event concept for a Shark Tank pitch. A date, venue, performers and suppliers have not been contracted yet.

Can I buy tickets or reserve a VIP table? +

Not yet. Ticket tiers and the ten VIP table upgrades are proposed offers, not active sales. Use the access buttons to view details.

Does the QR vote work on multiple phones? +

The QR opens this webpage on phones after publishing. Votes remain a local demo unless the optional Supabase shared-voting system is configured. See the GitHub README.

Are drinks, lighting and the floor plan confirmed? +

They are professional concepts, pending final agreements, quotes and venue-safety approval.
